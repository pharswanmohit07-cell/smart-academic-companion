One connected workspace

Tools for the everyday work of studying.

Practical utilities help turn academic information into clear next steps, without adding unnecessary complexity.

01 / ATTENDANCE

Safe Attendance Calculator

Understand your current attendance, calculate the classes needed to reach a target, and estimate how many upcoming classes you can miss.

02 / CAMPUS

Classroom Finder

Check room availability for a selected day and time, identify timetable clashes, and find open slots.

03 / STUDY

AI Study Planner

List your topics, set your preparation level and available time, then receive a prioritised study schedule.

04 / PROGRESS

Student Dashboard

Review attendance, recorded marks, assessments and academic updates in one personal overview.

05 / FACULTY

Academic Overview

Faculty can review student records, attendance, exam performance and incomplete academic information for their assigned classes.

06 / ORGANISATION

Class Management

Create classes, organise student lists and keep faculty records scoped to the classes they manage.

A straightforward workflow

Get from information to action.

The application is designed around the tasks students and faculty already need to complete.

01. Sign in securely

Use your account to open the workspace assigned to your role. New public registrations are student accounts.

02. Choose a task

Open the relevant tool to plan attendance, find a classroom, organise study time or review academic records.

03. Review your next step

Use clear calculations, schedules and academic summaries to decide what needs attention.

Designed for each role

Student tools and faculty oversight, kept in their place.

Each account sees the tools and records permitted for its role. Faculty-only operations are not exposed through public student registration.

For students

Stay on top of your own academic work.

Use the student workspace to understand your progress and plan the work ahead.

  • Attendance calculations and planning
  • Study schedules based on topic preparation
  • Personal marks and assessment status
  • Classroom availability tools
For faculty

Review records for the classes you manage.

Faculty access is provisioned by an administrator and scoped to assigned classes.

  • Attendance and assessment records
  • Midterm and end-term marks
  • Class and student organisation
  • Academic indicators for guidance, not automated decisions
